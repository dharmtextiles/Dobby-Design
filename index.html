Quick guide

The board

Top left: threading, with the denting row under it. Top right: tie-up, or shaft names when you use a peg plan. Below: the draw-down, the weft colours, and the treadling or peg plan with every pick numbered.

Entering a draft

  • Click or drag in the threading, tie-up and treadling grids.
  • Click or drag the colour bars to paint with the selected colour.
  • Pegplan menu switches between tie-up with treadling and a direct peg plan.

Editing the design

Click a cell in the Design pane or the draw-down to flip it. Your threading stays as it is and the peg plan changes to match. An end moves to a new shaft only when it must differ from the other ends on its shaft. Use Edit, Reduce shafts to merge identical ends.

Design pane

The Design pane on the right is always live. It is built from the draft and peg plan as you enter them. Black is warp on top. Click a cell in it (or in the draw-down) to flip it, and the threading and peg plan change to match. Design menu: invert, flip, clear, reduce shafts. Undo reverses any change.

Weaves

Insert menu, Weave library. Use a weave for the whole draft, or select a range first and fill just that area of the design. Save your own weaves there too.

Ranges

Drag across cells in the Design pane or the draw-down. The area stays selected when you let go, and a single click clears it. Use the Range and Repeat menus to repeat, mirror, duplicate, delete or paint it.

Keys

Ctrl+Z undo, Ctrl+Y redo, + − zoom, 0 fit, Esc clears the selection or closes a window.

Press and hold (or right-click) the image to save it.
